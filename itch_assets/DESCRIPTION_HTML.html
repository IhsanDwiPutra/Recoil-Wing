🦅 RECOIL WING — Cyber-Bird Action Simulator

Recoil Wing is an adrenaline-fueled 60 FPS retro-arcade roguelite shooter! Navigate perilous neon skyscraper sectors, obliterate rogue security drones with heavy downward recoil cannon fire, and blast through holographic in-flight choice gates to customize your tactical build in mid-air.

⚡ GAMEPLAY FEATURES:

  • 🚀 Downward Recoil Propulsion: Your primary weapon is your jet thruster! Blast downwards to propel yourself upward through narrow vertical conduits.
  • 🎯 Airborne Security Drones & Dual-Lane Corridors: Choose your route wisely—risk the combat drone lane for high scores and ammo refunds, or take the calm corridor.
  • 🔥 Arcade Combo Streak System: String consecutive drone eliminations to trigger score multipliers from x2 COMBO up to x5+ HYPER STREAK with luminous afterimage motion trails!
  • ⚡ Real-Time Choice Gates (No Pauses!): Fly directly through glowing portal gates to pick tactical perks:
    • 💥 Heavy Shotgun (Triple pellet spread)
    • ⚡ High-Velocity Piercer (Porous beam that penetrates solid crates)
    • 🛡️ Kinetic Energy Shield (Absorbs fatal impacts)
    • 🎾 Ricochet Bullets (Bounces off steel pipes)
    • 🔋 Expanded Magazine (6-round capacity with fast reload)
  • 🌧️ Dynamic Biome & Weather Shifts: Transition seamlessly between:
    • Sector 1: Neon Downtown (Midnight indigo & cyber cyan)
    • Sector 2: Acid Rain District (Atmospheric downpours, water splashes & thunderclaps)
    • Sector 3: Solar Overdrive (Crimson skies & floating solar embers)
  • ⏸️ Mobile & Desktop Pause Menu: In-game pause menu with independent BGM & SFX audio toggles and full touch-screen support.
  • 🏆 Persistent High Score: Compete against your personal best record saved automatically.

🎮 CONTROLS:

  • Blast / Jump: SPACEBAR, W, UP ARROW, or LEFT CLICK / TOUCH SCREEN TAP
  • Pause Game: ESC, P, or tap the on-screen ❚❚ Pause Button (Top-Right)
  • Restart: R or via Pause Menu
  • Audio Mute: M

📱 PLAY ANYWHERE:

Play directly inside your web browser on PC, Mac, Linux, Chromebook, Android, and iOS! Fully responsive with one-touch mobile controls.
